Zero-shot at 0.5 held back every stronger head; 0.25 (or 0 for frames alone) is better for them.

Why not the larger designs: the HUD corners are 144 x 135 pixels in the stored 480 x 270 thumbnails, blown up to 256, while a live frame gives
576 x 540 pixels: a different picture to the one the head learns from, for no gain in the filtered result. V-JEPA 2 / I-JEPA / VLMs: see
backbones.txt and vlm.txt. Temporal features were not tried: the stored frames are 0.5 to 6 s apart depending on the recording.

Rust replay (crates/openps-scene/tests/replay.rs, shipped model's out-of-recording readings, simulated presses):
  scene right: old filter 90.9 %, press-gated filter 91.7 % (was 86.0 % and 87.4 % with the first version's readings)
  the filter's vehicle parameters were swept again (replay.rs, ignored test): the best cell is 92.4 % against 91.7 % for the defaults, within
  the noise of five recordings, and trades staying on foot beside a vehicle for leaving a vehicle sooner; the defaults stay.

Live frames against training thumbnails (examples/read.rs on 1080p frames and their 480 px JPEGs, two recordings): cosine of the embeddings
0.97 to 0.99 (whole) and 0.96 to 0.99 (DINOv2-B), the same scene every time, so frames are not scaled down first.

Cost: both models through Vision/Core ML, 28 ms per picture in the test (file read included), one picture every 0.5 s while detection is on.
Core ML alone: MobileCLIP-S1 about 5 ms, DINOv2-B about 19 ms (experiments/verify_model.py checks the export: cosines to PyTorch 0.970 to 0.9999).

Size: the DINOv2-B package is 164 MB (16-bit weights). 8-bit weights make it 82 MB with the same speed (17 ms) and embeddings within cosine
0.9985 of the 16-bit ones, but need macOS 14 and the app supports 13 (scripts/install.sh), so it ships in 16 bits.

The scripts behind these tables are in experiments/ (frozen to the first ten recordings and the caches they made); scene.py eval is the
maintained evaluation of the shipped design, and picks up new recordings by itself.
